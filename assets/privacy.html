Privacy Policy

Clarity Caddy is operated by Sam Hale Golf in Auckland, New Zealand.

Clarity Caddy may collect information needed to provide app features, including account details, player profile information, golf bag distance setup, practice-data records, saved player data, GPS play information, payment access status, and support messages.

This information is used to provide and improve Clarity Caddy features, support player and coaching workflows, manage paid access, and respond to support requests.

Payment processing may be handled by third-party payment providers such as Stripe. Clarity Caddy does not need to store full card details on its own systems.

For privacy questions or support, contact Sam Hale Golf at samhalegolf@gmail.com.
