Golf GPS + coaching support

Clearer decisions before, during, and after the shot.

The app provides GPS-based course guidance, golf bag distance setup, player profile tools, practice-data features, and coaching-related decision support.

GPS play tools

Use course guidance and on-course tools to choose smarter targets and make clearer playing decisions.

Golf bag setup

Set up club distances so the app can support practical decisions around club choice and shot planning.

Practice-data tools

Use practice information to support coaching conversations and connect practice patterns to golf-course decisions.

What's in the app

Everything you expect from a GPS app — plus the part that's missing.

Distances, slope, wind and offline maps are table stakes. What Clarity adds is your own shot pattern, carried from practice onto the tee.

The GPS basics

  • ⌖Front, middle and back distances on every hole
  • △Slope-adjusted plays-like distance
  • ≋Live Wind speed and direction 
  • ⤓Map Library, downloadable for offline play
  • ▦Live Club Recomendations

What makes Clarity Caddy special

The Bubble system, and the loop around it.

  • ⇄Practice, Course and My Bubble compared in one view
  • %Course transfer score — how much of practice actually showed up
  • ⌸Carity finds the diamond in the rough in your practice data and gives you a way to use it.

Your bag, your numbers

Set once, used on every shot.

  • ✓Full bag setup with carry and total per club
  • ✓Club suggestion for the shot in front of you
  • ✓Launch monitor import — photo, file or typed in
  • ✓Saved player profile that travels between rounds
  • ✓Shot data shareable with your coach
