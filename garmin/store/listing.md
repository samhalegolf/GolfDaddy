## Description (4000 max)

```
Clarity Caddy puts your own shot data on your wrist.

The phone app builds your Bubble from real practice data — the zone your shots
actually finish in, not the one number on the side of the club. On the watch you
get the shot in front of you and the hole in front of you, without taking the
phone out of your pocket.

ON THE WATCH
• Front, centre and back distances to the green, and the club your bag covers
  the shot with.
• The hole map, with your position, the green, your aim point, and your Bubble
  drawn where it will actually land.
• Aim from the wrist. On touch watches, press and hold the map where you want to
  hit. On button watches, UP and DOWN move the target. Confirm and the phone
  follows.
• Take the round over from the watch, lock a shot as you play it, and move
  between holes.

REQUIRES THE CLARITY CADDY PHONE APP
This is a companion app — it has nothing of its own to show. Open a round in
Clarity Caddy on your iPhone or Android phone and the watch fills in. With no
round running on the phone, the watch shows "Waiting for round", which is the
app working correctly, not a fault. Watch play is free — no membership is
needed.

WHY IT ASKS FOR THESE PERMISSIONS
• Positioning (GPS): every distance on the screen is measured from where you are
  standing, and your position is what places you on the hole map and sizes your
  Bubble. Without GPS the app has no numbers to show.
• Communications: to exchange the round with the Clarity Caddy app on your phone
  over Connect IQ, and to fetch the hole map images. The watch app holds no
  account, no login, and sends nothing to anywhere but your own phone and the
  map image service.

SUPPORTED WATCHES
Approach S62, Approach S70 (42mm and 47mm), fenix 6, Forerunner 55.

More at https://claritygolf.app
```
